## 🔓 The main story: the worse_query hole in iOS

Researcher **forcequit** demonstrated a vulnerability with a memorable name — **worse_query**: an app can escape its sandbox and **read other apps' files** — passwords, keys and crypto wallet seed phrases. **Binance has already warned its users.**

Who should worry:

- **iOS 26 and 27.2 are vulnerable** — and note: the fresh 27.2.1 does **not** fix it either.
- App Store apps are reviewed by Apple; the real risk is for those who install **IPAs outside the store** (sideloading, ports, "cracked" clients).
- No actual thefts recorded yet — but the hole's potential is serious.

Practical advice if you hold crypto:

1. keep your wallet on a **separate phone**;
2. no sideloaded IPAs on the device that holds money — even from trusted developers;
3. **never store seed phrases** in notes or screenshots;
4. if you suspect your wallet was exposed — move funds to a new one, created on a clean device.

I'll post separately once Apple patches it.

## ✈️ Telegram Web /k: first update in two years

Telegram's web version has two official builds by separate teams: `/a` (the default) and `/k` — which just got its first update since 2024:

- new design and **four themes**;
- up to **3 accounts** in one tab (4 with Premium);
- photo and video editor;
- noise-suppressed calls that recover themselves after drops;
- a **passcode lock** — a must-have if your computer is shared;
- video circles, message translation, bot widgets and hundreds of fixes.

Switching is as easy as changing one letter: `web.telegram.org/k`.

## 📱 iPhone Duo vs the CNET machine: 457,000 folds

CNET has been folding an iPhone Duo with a purpose-built machine for over three weeks. The counter passed **457 thousand** — and the first problems appeared:

- after ~390 thousand the **hinge started loosening**;
- after ~440 thousand the screen stopped opening fully without extra force.

Wear math: at 100 folds a day the hinge lasts ~12 years. So the Duo's build is genuinely sturdy — but remember, some units' **screens crack on their own**, which Apple has already acknowledged.

## 🎮 GTA VI launches November 19 — Twitch is giving away keys

GTA VI releases **November 19** (PS5 and Xbox Series; no PC version at launch). At TwitchCon it was announced that from November 19–30, viewers of GTA VI streams can win **Ultimate Edition keys** — over 10 thousand of them. Terms and regions are still undisclosed. The odds are slim with millions watching, but a background stream costs nothing.
