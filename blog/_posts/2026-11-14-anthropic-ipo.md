In a few days, on **November 19, 2026**, Anthropic — the company behind Claude — starts trading on the New York Stock Exchange. By all forecasts it will be **the largest tech IPO in history**: the company's valuation may exceed a trillion dollars. The numbers, the context, and what could go wrong.

## The company

Anthropic was founded in 2021 by OpenAI defectors — Dario and Daniela Amodei plus five other co-founders (seven in total, and every one of them does nicely at the listing). Its signature ideology is "safety-first": the company positions itself as the most cautious of the AI labs. The product is the Claude model family — GPT's and Gemini's chief rival for enterprise customers.

A telling detail: this is actually a "two-figure" IPO. Alongside the U.S. prospectus, the company filed in Australia — the Australian offer opened November 11 and closes on the 17th. Big tech listings have never done that before.

## Why it's historic

For comparison, previous record-holders:

- **Facebook (2012)** — ~$104B valuation at IPO;
- **Alibaba (2014)** — a record $25B raised, ~$231B valuation;
- **Saudi Aramco (2019)** — $29.4B raised, but that's a state company.

Anthropic could start life worth **4–13x more than 2012's Facebook**. A five-year-old company joining the Apple/Microsoft/NVIDIA trillionaire club — in a single day.

And that's with the whole industry shaking this week: Huang's exit from NVIDIA has the compute suppliers rattled, and Anthropic is one of the largest chip buyers in the world. The market is still ready to pay. Demand for AI assets currently outweighs any scandal.

## What could go wrong

For fairness — three risks:

1. **Compute dependence.** Billions in revenue turn into billions in GPU and data-center bills. No one has proven AI-business margins over the long run yet.
2. **Competition isn't sleeping.** Google's Gemini 4 Carbon costs half of Claude at comparable benchmarks — the price war eats margins faster than the customer base grows.
3. **Overheating.** A $916B–$1.4T valuation range is half a trillion dollars of spread. When analysts themselves can't agree on the price, day one can be violent in either direction.

## What to watch on November 19

- **The first candle**: the premium over $70–78 shows how hungry the market really is for AI assets.
- **A vote for the whole industry**: ANT's success wakes up the IPO queue — several AI companies are reportedly lined up behind Anthropic.
- **NVIDIA and Broadcom's reaction**: capital that flows into ANT is capital that could have flowed into chipmakers.

My forecast: the offering prices above the middle of the range, the first week is volatile, and a quarter from now nobody remembers IPO day — everyone stares at a single chart: revenue growth versus compute-cost growth. That chart decides whether this day was historic or just expensive.
